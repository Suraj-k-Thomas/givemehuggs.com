01 How it works

Three taps. That's the whole thing.

No accounts to wrangle, no monthly commitment, no awkward maths. Find the person whose work you enjoy and send them something real.

  1. 1

    Find your creator

    Search by name or open the link they shared. Artists, writers, developers, musicians — anyone doing work you value.

  2. 2

    Pick an amount, add a note

    $1 to say hello, $25 to say thank you properly. Attach a message or an emoji so it lands as more than a number.

  3. 3

    They get it instantly

    Your Huggs arrives with a real-time notification, and your message arrives with it. That's the whole flow.

07 Questions

Good things to know

What exactly is a "Huggs"?

A Huggs is a small cash gift — anything from $1 upwards — sent to a creator, optionally with a message. It's a one-tap way to say "this was worth something to me".

Do I need an account to send one?

You can send a Huggs from a creator's link in a few taps. An account only becomes useful when you want to keep a history, set up monthly support, or follow creators.

What does it cost creators?

A small transaction fee of 5–10% per Huggs, shown transparently before payout. Optional premium tools and promotion are separate, and always opt-in.

Is it safe?

Payments are encrypted and processed through established payment providers. We don't store raw card details, and supporter privacy settings are respected on every gift.

Which countries are supported?

The platform is built to work worldwide, with payout availability rolling out region by region. Check the creator sign-up flow for the current list.
